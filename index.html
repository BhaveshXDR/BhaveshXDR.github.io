01 about.md // who's behind the terminal

I'm a Cybersecurity & Data Science undergraduate at Garden City University, Bengaluru, currently building hands-on experience across offensive and defensive security. My focus areas span Linux systems, network security, OSINT reconnaissance, and vulnerability assessment — with a growing interest in Extended Detection & Response (XDR) and threat analysis workflows.

I train regularly on TryHackMe (handle: BhaveshXDR), working through red-vs-blue scenarios, privilege escalation, and defensive-tooling challenges to sharpen both attack and detection skills.
